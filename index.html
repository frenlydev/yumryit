03 · Our Story

From the hills,
with intention.

YUMRYIT is a boutique label rooted in Darjeeling and created by Amrita Chettri. The brand brings together a love of design, colour, texture and the everyday rituals that make us feel a little more ourselves.

From a signature occasionwear silhouette to a candle that looks like your favourite drink, YUMRYIT is about making beautiful things that invite you to pause, enjoy and make them your own.
