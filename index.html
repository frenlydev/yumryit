03 · Our Story

Made from a life lived widely.

YUMRYIT was born from Amrita Chettri's love for beautiful things, thoughtful details and the simple act of taking time for oneself.

A former airhostess who lived outside India for more than half her life, Amrita came home with a renewed appreciation for the calm, colour and character of the hills. Today, she creates YUMRYIT as a reminder that self-love does not need to be grand or complicated. In an age of chaos and “no-time-for-self”, sometimes it begins with one beautiful thing chosen just for you.
